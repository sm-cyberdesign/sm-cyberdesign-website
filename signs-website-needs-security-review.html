7 Signs Your Business Website Needs a Security Review

Small businesses benefit from straightforward technology practices that are easy to understand and maintain. This guide focuses on useful steps, not complicated terminology.

In this guide
  1. You are unsure who has administrator access
  2. Plugins or website software are rarely updated
  3. Your forms collect more information than necessary
  4. Nobody has tested restoring a backup
  5. The website relies on several unknown third parties
  6. Visitors report confusing errors or broken pages
  7. Your business or website has changed substantially

1. You are unsure who has administrator access

Review active users, permission levels, and whether former vendors or staff still have access.

2. Plugins or website software are rarely updated

An update and dependency inventory helps you understand maintenance needs.

3. Your forms collect more information than necessary

Check whether every field is needed and where submissions are stored.

4. Nobody has tested restoring a backup

Confirm that backups exist and that restoration works.

5. The website relies on several unknown third parties

Identify embedded widgets, analytics, forms, and integrations and who maintains them.

6. Visitors report confusing errors or broken pages

Usability problems may hide configuration or workflow issues worth investigating.

7. Your business or website has changed substantially

A new payment flow, customer portal, hosting provider, or team may justify reviewing assumptions and controls.
