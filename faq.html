Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about secure websites, technology support, and practical security services.

Does my small business need cybersecurity guidance?

Businesses of many sizes rely on websites, accounts, email, and customer information. Practical safeguards can help reduce avoidable risks.

Can you review an existing website?

Yes. We can discuss an agreed review scope and provide prioritized observations and next steps.

Do you design websites as well as advise on security?

Yes. Our work combines accessible website design, usable digital experiences, and practical security considerations.

Can you help if we do not have an IT department?

We offer scoped technology support and guidance for small organizations. Availability and support arrangements are confirmed before work begins.

How much does a security review cost?

Pricing depends on the systems involved and the depth of the review. A discovery conversation helps establish scope and an estimate.

Can you help with customer forms and privacy?

We can review form usability and information collection practices. For legal compliance determinations, consult a qualified attorney.
