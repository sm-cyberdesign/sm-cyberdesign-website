How to Make a Business Website Accessible and Secure

Small businesses benefit from straightforward technology practices that are easy to understand and maintain. This guide focuses on useful steps, not complicated terminology.

In this guide
  1. Make important tasks easy to complete
  2. Design forms for real people
  3. Avoid unnecessary friction
  4. Respect customer information
  5. Test across devices and abilities
  6. Maintain the experience

1. Make important tasks easy to complete

Use descriptive headings, understandable navigation, and clear calls to action so visitors can find information without unnecessary steps.

2. Design forms for real people

Provide labels, helpful instructions, keyboard support, and error messages that explain how to recover.

3. Avoid unnecessary friction

Security controls should be proportionate to risk. Consider accessible authentication options and avoid interactions that depend only on visual cues.

4. Respect customer information

Limit data collection, use appropriate access controls, and explain how information is handled.

5. Test across devices and abilities

Review mobile layouts, zoom, keyboard navigation, screen-reader behavior, and reduced-motion settings. Automated checks alone are not enough.

6. Maintain the experience

Recheck accessibility and security after content, plugin, and workflow changes.
